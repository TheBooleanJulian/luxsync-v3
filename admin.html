Galleries needing attention

Galleries the background mirror hasn't finished copying into R2. Until it has, those files come from Google Drive and can hit its limits.

    Prepare gallery

    Every gallery that gets opened is copied into LuxSync's own storage automatically in the background (every 5 minutes, including files added later), so clients never hit Google Drive's limits. Use Prepare to do it right now, before you share the link. Safe to re-run: files already copied are skipped. Originals of a gallery nobody opens for 30 days are deleted (thumbnails and previews stay), and copied again the next time it's opened.

      Mirror new galleries automatically

      Add the folder you deliver galleries into (for example #CLIENT_DELIVERABLES/2026). Every 15 minutes LuxSync looks for new gallery folders inside it and copies them into R2, so they're ready before you send the link. Folders that were already there when you add it are left alone. Reading a private folder needs the owner sign-in (see Health → Setup).
